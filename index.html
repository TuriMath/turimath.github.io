About

TuriMath is a Turi Labs factory, the second after TuriChips. One human sets the direction; the agents do the work. The first goal is 40 machine-checked theorems and an open environment for training AI to do formal maths.

The proofs live in TuriMath/theorems. Anyone can check them: clone it and run scripts/gate.sh.
